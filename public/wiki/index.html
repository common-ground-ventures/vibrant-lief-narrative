Lïef Development and the build-it-ourselves model

A luxury developer and builder in Phoenix that draws what it builds and owns what it draws, with its own estimating engine and a brand system split by audience.

What Lïef Development is

Lïef Development + Construction is a luxury residential developer and builder in Arizona. Its home page calls it "Arizona's Premier Luxury Home Builder" and puts the whole model in one line: we draw it, we build it, we own it [1]. Architecture, construction and development sit in one team instead of three vendors passing a project between them, and the site describes the result as one team with no gaps [1].

The firm designs and builds high-end custom homes. Through its sister company Lïef Builders it also provides owner's representation, architecture, preconstruction and general contracting nationally [7]. Its estimating practice runs on Kanopi, an engine the firm built for its own bids and now shares with other builders [8]. Common Ground's wiki lists Lïef as a principal holding of the group and the anchor of its real estate practice [7].

The case for the model is the one the site makes in three words: no gaps. When the people who draw a project, price it and build it sit on one team, there are fewer handoffs for cost and intent to leak through. Lïef keeps design, structure, construction and brand in one room.

The model: draw it, build it, own it

The site lists what the team does in house: architectural design, structural engineering, general construction, custom millwork, electrical systems, plumbing and mechanical, HVAC engineering, interior design, landscape architecture, smart home integration, custom stone and tile, and fine finishing [1]. It describes the design approach as starting with a problem to solve, not a style to copy, and names the design team as Scott Meiers, chief architectural design, with MOR Studio grounding the work in research on how people live and move through spaces [1].

Labor comes through Armstrong Construction Group, which the Common Ground record calls Lïef's other managing partner and which brings its own in-house crews [9]. The site's footer invites three kinds of partner: developers, a managing general contractor and distributors [1].

The firm works on three kinds of ground. The design language on its site blends Japanese tranquility, Scandinavian minimalism and Sonoran serenity [1]. The first is the custom luxury home in Phoenix, Scottsdale and Paradise Valley. The second is adaptive reuse and infill, where an existing building becomes something new. The third is boutique residential communities and multifamily [1][2].

Projects the site lists

The projects page lists five current projects [2].

  • 301 W Osborn, Midtown Phoenix. An adaptive reuse conversion of a commercial building into luxury urban residences with secured parking and conditioned storage [2]. This is Lïef's own project and the deepest record in the group; the full story is on the Lïef Midtown wiki and in the hub entry [4].
  • The Vail. A boutique 55-plus community in Arizona, listed as in development [2].
  • Canyon Corporate Plaza, Phoenix. An office-to-residential conversion. Lïef's side of the Common Ground record is the takeoff and pricing of the interior partition package on Xtrata's SABS wall system, a package priced at $2,041,596 for Building 1 [6].
  • Silver Mountain Ranches. An equestrian community the site lists as in development [2].
  • 440 Basin Avenue, Pahrump, Nevada. A 204-unit multifamily development [2].

Other work on the Common Ground record includes a Ghana master plan proposal, a hotel feasibility study and a Phoenix creative district development lead [7].

Two brands, one rule

Lïef runs two visual systems on purpose. Lïef Development is the company brand, built for investors, developers, general contractors and trade press, in deep green with a neon green accent, set in Archivo Black and DM Sans. The project brand, used for Lïef Midtown, is built for home buyers, design press and agents, in warm earth tones set in Japandi [5]. A written decision table assigns each kind of content to one system, with the rule that the two are never blended in one asset [5].

The neon accent is held to a small share of any layout and never fills a background [3]. The spelling rule is just as plain: the name carries the diaeresis in all brand copy and drops it only in domains, email addresses and handles [5]. An outside marketing agency that reviewed the system in April 2026 called the split "one of the smarter structural decisions we've seen in a project brief" [5]. The brand system was a team effort across the principals; Jesse Fowler supplied the name Lïef and the narrative the brand is built around, and Jimmy Khounlavong worked on the identity, the guide and the sites [5][3].

The site that this wiki sits on follows the company brand. It was generated in an AI site builder in early 2026 and then worked over for search and AI readiness: structured data, crawler rules, a sitemap, canonical addresses and a plain-text fact sheet for AI engines [3].

Building technology: SABS

The site's SABS page presents Lïef x SABS as its structural edge [1]. SABS is the Saebi Alternative Building System, a patented three-layer composite that Xtrata makes: EPS foam panels with a high-strength concrete composite coating, replacing wood framing [10]. Published performance figures include a 0% flame spread rating, resistance to 250 mph hurricane and tornado winds, insulation values of R-75 to R-100, a Sound Transmission Class of 52 and 100% recyclable materials [10].

Lïef's part is installation and the bid desk. On the Common Ground record, Lïef prices and bids installation of SABS on the jobs Xtrata's channel sends, and Jesse Fowler's seat with Xtrata has run since 2025 [10]. The site says Lïef builds across wood frame, steel and advanced methods as well, and offers SABS as one more high-performance option so the client can match the system to the project [1]. The how and the why of the system are on the Lïef x SABS wiki.

AI oversight and estimating

The site's AI page says the team is trained in artificial intelligence and machine learning tools and lists eight uses: change order management and negotiation, plan and specification clarification, estimating verification and audit, budget tracking and variance analysis, schedule optimization and monitoring, document analysis and compliance review, vendor and material price verification, and insurance claim documentation [4]. It reports an average of 14% time savings across a project timeline [4].

The deepest example is Kanopi. It reads a plan set, takes off the quantities, prices them from a sourced rate library and assembles a bid, with guards that refuse to ship a number the engine cannot stand behind [8]. As of September 25, 2026 it is in place with eight builders and developers pricing their own work, with over $600 million in proposals bid through it nationally in 2026 [8]. Its public accuracy claim is narrow by design: priced blind across three single-family bids, mean error of 18.6 percent, worst bid 31.9 percent, measured June 10, 2026 [8].

Who it serves

Lïef serves four groups. Home buyers and owners who want a custom luxury home or a residence in a converted building. Investors in its own projects. Developers and general contractors who want an estimating engine or an installer for a panel system. And property owners who need an advocate on the other side of a build, through Lïef Builders [1][7].

Frequently asked questions

What is Lïef Development? Lïef Development + Construction is a luxury residential developer and builder in Phoenix, Arizona, and the company behind Lïef Midtown at 301 W Osborn. It designs, builds and owns its projects, and works in custom homes, adaptive reuse and boutique multifamily [1][2][4].

Who runs Lïef Development? Jesse Fowler is principal and manager, working with Jon Armstrong of Armstrong Construction Group on construction, Scott Meiers on architectural design and Jimmy Khounlavong on brand [3][7].

What is the difference between Lïef Development and Lïef Builders? Lïef Development develops and builds its own projects. Lïef Builders works for property owners: project advocacy and oversight, owner's representation, preconstruction and general contracting, nationally [7]. Its own wiki is at liefbuilders.com/wiki.

How is Lïef spelled? Lïef is written with a diaeresis over the i in all brand copy. The mark is two leaves dotting the i. The diaeresis is dropped only in domains, email addresses and handles, which is why the web address is anewlief.com [5].

What is SABS and does Lïef use it? SABS is the Saebi Alternative Building System, a patented three-layer composite of EPS foam panels and a concrete composite coating, made by Xtrata. Lïef prices and installs it and offers it next to conventional framing [1][10].

What is Lïef's estimating engine? It is called Kanopi. It takes off quantities from a plan set and prices them from a sourced rate library. As of September 25, 2026 it was in use at eight builders and developers [8].

Who built this website? Common Ground created it, with Jimmy Khounlavong doing the search and AI readiness work in 2026 [3]. The credit line in the footer reads "Created by Common Ground" [1].

Where is Lïef Midtown? 301 W Osborn Road in Midtown Phoenix, in a 1971 office tower being converted into 22 residences [4].
